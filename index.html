School of Ministry

The Light and Its Carrier School of Ministry and Christian Leadership Training

Welcome to the School of Ministry

This school equips believers to know the Word, walk in the Spirit, and serve with excellence. Whether you are called to lead, teach, or serve behind the scenes, there is a place for you.

Apply or log in to reach your own student or teacher portal, with live classes, lessons, a library, a reading room, practice questions, and more.

How it works

  • Apply on this website
  • Log in to your own member or teacher portal
  • Learn through materials, live classes, and assignments
  • Receive your certificate after six months

What you get

📖

Theology materials

Structured courses and lessons from our teachers.

🎥

Live classes

Join teachers by video or audio, with recordings to rewatch if you miss a class.

📚

Ministry library

Christian books and audios, free for teachers and unlocked with points for students.

✍️

Practice and assignments

Practice questions for every course, plus assignments marked by your teachers.

🙏

Prayer and events

Send prayer requests, join fellowship chats, and see ministry events.

🎓

Certificate

Awarded after six months of training.

Your learning path

Six courses take you step by step through the Word, prayer, and ministry.

1

Foundations of Faith

Salvation, the Word, and the basics of the Christian life.

2

Prayer and Intercession

How to pray, fast, and stand in the gap for others.

3

Prophetic Ministry

Hearing God’s voice and ministering the prophetic with wisdom.

4

Christian Leadership

Servant leadership, character, and serving the church.

5

Bible Survey

A walk through the Old and New Testaments.

6

Evangelism and Discipleship

Sharing the gospel and helping new believers grow.

For students

  • Join your live classes on time and keep a notebook.
  • Do the practice questions after every class. Each new correct answer earns a point for the Library.
  • Read a Reading room post every day.
  • Submit assignments before the deadline and read your teacher’s comments.
  • Listen to the recording of any class you missed.
  • Keep training for six months to earn your certificate.

For teachers

  • Create your live class from the Teaching desk and add it to the timetable.
  • Post lessons, documents, and videos for each course.
  • Press Record during the class to save an audio recording for students who miss it.
  • Add practice questions and assignments, then mark submissions with comments.
  • Write devotionals and teachings for the Reading room.
  • Pray for your students and follow up on those who miss classes.

Answer the practice questions and submit any assignment.

Questions and answers

Who can apply?

Any believer who wants to grow in the Word. Choose student or teacher when you apply.

How do teachers get approved?

Teachers apply like everyone else. The school reviews each application and opens the teaching tools once approved.

What are points?

Students earn points by answering practice questions and reading Reading room posts. Points unlock books and audios in the Library.

When do I get my certificate?

After six months of training. You can print it from your portal.
